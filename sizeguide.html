Sizing Tips

True to Size

The Air Max 95 generally fits true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend going half a size up.

Wide Feet

If you have wider feet, consider going up half a size for a more comfortable fit.

Measure Your Foot

For the most accurate fit, measure the length of your foot from heel to toe and compare it to the chart below.
