About House of 95s

House of 95s is an independent online sneaker store dedicated to the sale and resale of Nike Air Max 95 sneakers. The brand is built around the cultural influence, design legacy, and timeless appeal of the Air Max 95, offering curated pairs for sneaker enthusiasts, collectors, and fashion-conscious buyers.

The Legacy of the 95

First released in 1995, the Nike Air Max 95 became one of the most recognizable silhouettes in sneaker culture. Known for its anatomy-inspired design, layered upper, and visible Air units, the shoe evolved from a performance runner into a streetwear icon. At House of 95s, this legacy shapes the identity of the brand and influences the visual storytelling of the website.
